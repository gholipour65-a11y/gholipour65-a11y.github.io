About Me

I am a researcher in photovoltaic semiconductors and solid-state physics, currently working in the perovskite photovoltaics field at the University of Stuttgart. My interests include perovskite solar-cell materials, tandem architectures, interface engineering, and stability testing. I value interdisciplinary collaboration between academic research and technology development.

My work has taken me from Alzahra University and Sharif University of Technology to EPFL, the Adolphe Merkle Institute, the University of Tehran and METU, and now to the Saliba Lab in Stuttgart. I received the Swiss Government Excellence Scholarship, gave a keynote at the Nano-Structured Solar Cells conference (2020), organized conferences and workshops, and have supervised junior researchers on perovskite interfaces and carbon quantum dots.
