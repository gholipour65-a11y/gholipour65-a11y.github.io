About Me

I am a researcher in photovoltaic semiconductors and solid-state physics, currently working in the perovskite photovoltaics field at the University of Stuttgart. My interests include perovskite solar-cell materials, tandem architectures, interface engineering, and stability testing. I value interdisciplinary collaboration between academic research and technology development.
